Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026 · nabemall (Choongwon Lee, Republic of Korea)

The short version

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no login. We do not collect anything from you when you browse it.

When you buy

Purchases are processed by our merchant of record, Gumroad, on their own infrastructure. They collect the details needed to take payment, issue a receipt and meet tax obligations. We receive only the order record they pass to us — typically the buyer's email address, the product bought, and the amount. We use that solely to deliver the file, answer support email and keep our own sales records.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share buyer data with anyone. We do not build advertising profiles, and we run no third-party trackers on this site.

Hosting

The pages are served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes the request logs needed to serve traffic; that processing is theirs, under their own policy.

Retention and your rights

Order records are kept while they are needed for accounting and support. You can ask us at kanno321@gmail.com for a copy of what we hold about you, or to delete it, and we will act on it. Records the merchant of record must retain by law stay with them.
