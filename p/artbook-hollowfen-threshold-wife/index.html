Why the prompts are included

Most character packs hand you pictures and stop. The problem with pictures is that you always need one more angle than you were given. This book includes the identity block the illustrations were generated from, so you can make the next view yourself, in your own tool, and have it still be the same person.

That block is the working part. It is written the way production identity locks have to be written — positive counts rather than negations, equipment placement stated per view — because that is what holds a figure together across pages.
