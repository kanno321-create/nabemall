Method

What “measured” means here.

  1. Spent, then written

    Each rule started as an order that failed and cost credits. Nothing here was reasoned out in advance and published as advice.

  2. Re-measurable

    Models invert behaviour between versions, so the rules are written as properties with a procedure for re-measuring them, not as a fixed ranking.

  3. Built before sold

    Buyers starting from scratch installed the packs and produced finished videos with them. Defects they hit were fixed before release.

These are methodologies performed by Claude Code, not programs that run themselves. You supply the generation credits.
