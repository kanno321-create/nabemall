What will this episode cost in credits?

Enter the shape of the episode. The rates are what our own account was billed across six months of production, not a vendor price page.

The billing unit is where the money leaks

Kling bills per second. Seedance bills in fixed 5-second and 10-second units. That single difference creates a dead zone: a cut between 4.6s and 8.5s on a Seedance tier is billed as a 10-second order, so roughly half of what you pay for is discarded at the edit. Writing a 5-second cut instead of a 6-second one is free.

Why this page will not convert credits to dollars for you

What a credit costs depends on which top-up it came from. Publishing an exchange rate we cannot document would be the one invented number on a site whose whole claim is that its numbers are measured. Enter your own and the tool will use it.
